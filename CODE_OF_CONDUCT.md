# Community Conduct Standard

NeuroInsight AI welcomes respectful, evidence-based collaboration from students, researchers, engineers, reviewers, and users. Participants are expected to communicate professionally, respect privacy and intellectual-property boundaries, and discuss research limitations honestly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, disclosure of private data or secrets, and deliberate misrepresentation of model capability are not acceptable. Disagreements should focus on evidence, code, documentation, and reproducible results.

For conduct concerns, use an owner-approved private contact channel. Do not disclose sensitive information in public reports. Maintainers may remove content or limit participation where necessary to protect participants, the project, or its users.
