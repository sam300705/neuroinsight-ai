# Product Strategy — Research Sandbox

NeuroInsight AI’s near-term product is an explainable MRI-AI research sandbox for students, teaching labs, and researchers who need to inspect a carefully bounded experimental classifier rather than a clinical workflow.

| Element | Product position |
|---|---|
| Core problem | Research demonstrations often hide model provenance, calibration, abstention, and limits. |
| Differentiated value | Makes fixed-split evidence, experimental confidence, failure boundaries, Grad-CAM attribution, and unavailable capabilities visible. |
| MVP journey | Learn → explore approved samples when licensed → analyse an authorised research image → understand evidence → optionally save derived artifacts → review/delete account history. |
| Non-clinical boundary | No diagnosis, patient-care workflow, risk score, treatment advice, segmentation mask, physical measurement, or 3D geometry. |
| Validation plan | Conduct owner-approved interviews/workshops with students and instructors; measure comprehension of limitations, successful safe uploads, and whether users distinguish attribution from segmentation. |
| Future commercial hypotheses | Hosted university workspaces, teaching workshops, and self-hosted institutional support only after real-user, legal, and ownership review. No billing is implemented. |
