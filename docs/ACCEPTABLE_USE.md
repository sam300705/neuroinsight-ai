# Acceptable Use — Draft for Owner/Legal Review

NeuroInsight AI is an explainable MRI-AI **research sandbox**. It is for students, teaching laboratories, researchers, and demonstrations of responsible engineering. It is not a diagnosis, screening, triage, treatment, radiology, or patient-care system.

> “This system is not a medical diagnosis and must not replace a qualified radiologist.”

Users must upload only material they are authorised to use and must not upload patient records, personal medical information, private DICOM studies, credentials, or restricted datasets. Users must not represent experimental model output, Grad-CAM attribution, confidence scores, or saved reports as clinical findings.

Mode B segmentation, volume, physical measurement, and 3D geometry are unavailable. Attempts to present unavailable functionality as a real result are prohibited.

This draft requires owner and legal review before it is relied upon as a public policy.
